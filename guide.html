🛡️ Quick Emergency Steps

1️⃣ Stay Calm

Stay calm and quickly understand what is happening before taking action.

2️⃣ Move to Safety

Move away from immediate danger and go to a safer location when possible.

3️⃣ Call for Help

Contact the appropriate emergency service and clearly explain the situation and your location.

4️⃣ Follow Instructions

Follow instructions given by emergency responders and official authorities.

📱 How Mobile Technology Helps

📞 Emergency Calls

A mobile phone can help you contact emergency services when immediate assistance is required.

📍 GPS Location

GPS can help identify your location and make it easier to communicate where help is needed.

📢 Emergency Alerts

Mobile alerts can provide important warnings and instructions during emergencies.
