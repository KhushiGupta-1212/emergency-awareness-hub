🛡️ Emergency Safety Tips

Following basic safety practices can help people stay prepared and respond more effectively during emergency situations.

🔋 Keep Your Phone Charged

Keep your mobile phone sufficiently charged so that you can communicate when emergency assistance is needed.

📞 Save Emergency Contacts

Save important emergency numbers and trusted contacts in your phone for quick access.

📍 Enable Location Services

Keep GPS and location services available when required so that your location can be shared with trusted people.

📢 Check Emergency Alerts

Pay attention to official emergency alerts and follow the safety instructions provided by authorities.

📱 Keep Important Information Ready

Store important information such as emergency contacts and essential medical or identification details safely.

🔐 Protect Personal Information

Share your location and personal information only with trusted people or appropriate emergency services.

⏱️ Before, During & After an Emergency

🟢 Before an Emergency

  • Keep your phone charged.
  • Save important emergency contacts.
  • Keep emergency alerts enabled.
  • Know basic safety procedures.

🟡 During an Emergency

  • Stay calm and assess the situation.
  • Move to a safer location if necessary.
  • Contact appropriate emergency services.
  • Follow official instructions.

🔵 After an Emergency

  • Stay in a safe location.
  • Check official updates.
  • Inform trusted people if needed.
  • Follow further instructions from authorities.
