🚨 Emergency Awareness

Emergency awareness means understanding how to respond safely and quickly when an unexpected emergency occurs.

Mobile technology can help people communicate, share their location, receive alerts and access important safety information during emergencies.

📱 Mobile Technology

Smartphones provide useful tools such as calls, messages, GPS, emergency alerts and safety applications.

📍 Location Sharing

Location sharing can help trusted people or emergency responders understand where assistance may be needed.

📢 Emergency Alerts

Emergency alerts can provide important information about dangerous situations and official warnings.

📞 Quick Communication

A mobile phone can help people quickly contact emergency services or trusted contacts when necessary.

✅ Do's & ❌ Don'ts During an Emergency

✅ Do's

  • Stay calm and assess the situation.
  • Contact the appropriate emergency service when needed.
  • Share your location with a trusted person.
  • Follow instructions from official authorities.
  • Move to a safe place when necessary.

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't panic or spread unverified information.
  • Don't take unnecessary risks.
  • Don't block emergency service routes.
  • Don't ignore official warnings.
  • Don't share unverified emergency information.
